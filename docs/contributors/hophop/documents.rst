Documents in hophop
===================

Documents in hophop that can be useful to Opener are:

* `the glossary <https://github.com/ariel-os/hophop/blob/main/doc/glossary.md>`__,
  merging relevant definitions from the ETSI specification
  with terminology from IETF (relevant for key establishment in joining)
  and occasionally own conventions (such as which terms to avoid due to potential for confusion)
  and own terms (eg. describing Nordic's IPv6 workaround).

  With some extra metadata on which term is relevant where,
  this could migrate into Opener.

* `external documents <https://github.com/ariel-os/hophop/blob/main/doc/external.md>`__
  lists and links relevant ETSI documents
  and references relevant highlights.

* The `rest of the documentation <https://github.com/ariel-os/hophop/tree/main/doc>`__
  includes pointers to obtaining and setting up then nRF9151 DECT firmware,
  and includes the READMEs of `examples <https://github.com/ariel-os/hophop/tree/main/examples>`__
  and `applications <https://github.com/ariel-os/hophop/tree/main/applications>`__.
